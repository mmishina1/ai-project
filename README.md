# AI. A Biography

An informational website that tells the story of artificial intelligence as a biography: where it came from (1950–2016), what it is now (benefits and risks) and who it might become (possible futures).

**Live site:** https://mmishina1.github.io/ai-project/

## Files

- `index.html` – the page (semantic sections, chapter windows as native `<dialog>` elements)
- `styles.css` – all styles, responsive down to phone width
- `script.js` – chapter cards, draggable photo album, “Be the AI” game, ending picker, sources popups
- `assets/` – photos used on the site (credits are listed in the Sources section of the site)

## Sources and image credits

All facts and image credits are listed in the **Sources** section at the bottom of the site. Photos are from Wikimedia Commons, Pixabay, PAKUTASO and a DTIC public report; Creative Commons images are credited with author and licence.
